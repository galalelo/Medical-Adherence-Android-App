SAFETY SUPPORT

Possible Overdose Report

Record what happened and get connected to official emergency guidance.

If the person collapses, has a seizure, has trouble breathing, or cannot be awakened, call emergency services immediately.

This form is not a diagnosis and must not delay emergency care.

For a suspected poisoning in the United States, Poison Control is free and confidential at 1-800-222-1222. Use webPOISONCONTROL when appropriate. Outside the U.S., use your local poison center or emergency number.
